**1. 输入与范围边界模糊**

**2. 核心概念未定义**
- "Tree assignment of limbs with length proportions rounded to whole numbers"——这段话只有熟悉 Treemaker 的人才能看懂。谁是这个文档的读者？是否需要补充简明定义？

- Step 2 的开头是 "Research on, and build..."，Step 3 用词是 "Conclude some..."。这些是开放的探索性工作，难以定义"完成"。建议将研究阶段与构建阶段分开，或为研究阶段设定明确交付物（如技术报告、PoC 原型、评估基准）。

### Research notes (grounding the decisions)
- **FOLD format** — this is the Origami Simulator's JSON standard (Amanda Ghassaei); adopting it gives you a free, tested fold simulation engine to build step 4 on instead of writing one from scratch.
- **TreeMaker** — Robert Lang's tool proves the tree→crease-pattern mapping works; your differentiator is the box-pleating constraint, which maps naturally to grid-based circle/river packing.
- **Flat-foldability** — Maekawa's theorem (M − V = 2) and Kawasaki's theorem (alternating angle sum = 180°) are cheap, exact checks; use them before any 3D simulation.

### Remaining minor items (only if you care later)

- The validation set's 15 photos will need to be collected and stored somewhere in-repo — worth adding a `validation/` folder when you start step 1.
